# HAMi Ambassador Program

**Maintained by**: HAMi Maintainer Team and Community Committee

## 1. Program Overview

HAMi (Heterogeneous AI Computing Virtualization Middleware) is an open source project for heterogeneous computing virtualization and scheduling in the cloud native space. As the community grows rapidly around the world, we need a group of active, trusted, and passionate community leaders to help more developers learn about, adopt, and contribute to HAMi.

The **HAMi Ambassador Program** is established following the proven model of the [CNCF Ambassador Program](https://www.cncf.io/people/ambassadors/). It aims to recognize and empower individuals who make sustained contributions to the HAMi community. Ambassadors are an "extension" of the HAMi community: through content creation, event organization, technical evangelism, and newcomer onboarding, they help bring heterogeneous computing management technology to more developers.

### What an Ambassador Is

- An outstanding representative of community contributors, officially recognized by the community
- A bridge between HAMi and developers around the world
- A voluntary community role with **no compensation**

### What an Ambassador Is Not

- Not a HAMi Maintainer / Reviewer (the two are independent systems; one may hold both, but neither grants the other)
- Not an employment relationship, and does not represent the commercial interests of any single company
- Not authorized to make official statements on behalf of the HAMi project without community approval

## 3. Ambassador Responsibilities

Ambassadors are expected to act in a community capacity during their term, in directions including but not limited to:

- **Content creation**: Write HAMi-related blogs, tutorials, and deep dives; produce videos and live-streamed courses
- **Event organization and speaking**: Organize meetups and online sessions; speak at conferences (KubeCon, KCD, CNCG, etc.)
- **Community operations**: Answer questions on Slack / WeChat groups / GitHub Discussions; help triage issues and onboard newcomers
- **Documentation and localization**: Improve official docs and provide multilingual translations
- **Ecosystem evangelism**: Promote the integration and adoption of HAMi with upstream and downstream projects (the Kubernetes ecosystem, GPU vendor ecosystems, AI frameworks, etc.)
- **Feedback loop**: Collect community and user feedback, and sync it with the Maintainer Team on a regular basis

### Minimum Activity Requirements

- Complete **at least 4 verifiable community activities every 12 months** during the term (content, talks, event organization, Q&A, etc. all count; self-reported by the Ambassador)
- Submit a brief **annual activity review** each year (used for the annual review, see Section 5)
- Comply with the [CNCF Code of Conduct](https://github.com/cncf/foundation/blob/main/code-of-conduct.md) and the HAMi Ambassador Code of Conduct (effective upon signature)

## 4. How to Become a HAMi Ambassador

### 4.1 Basic Requirements (all must be met)

1. At least 18 years old;
2. Identify with open source values, and agree to and sign the HAMi Ambassador Code of Conduct;
3. Have a **verifiable track record of contributions** to the HAMi community within the past 12 months.

### 4.2 Contribution Areas (at least two must be met)

Following the CNCF model, meeting **two or more** of the following areas qualifies you to apply:

| # | Area | Examples |
| :-- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 | Code / documentation contributions | PRs, reviews, and issue contributions to HAMi and ecosystem repositories (HAMi, website, HAMi-DRA, etc.) |
| 2 | Content creation | Blogs, videos, tutorials, live streams, technical media articles (must reflect the open source spirit and not be paywalled) |
| 3 | Event organization | Organizing HAMi-related meetups or community gatherings, or helping organize KCD / CNCG and similar events |
| 4 | Public speaking | Talks on HAMi / heterogeneous computing topics at industry conferences or community events |
| 5 | Community operations | Long-term Q&A, issue triage, newcomer onboarding, documentation translation and localization |
| 6 | Mentoring experience | Serving as a mentor for LFX, GSoC, OSPP (Summer of Open Source), or similar programs with HAMi as the target project |
| 7 | Ecosystem adoption | Driving real-world adoption of HAMi at your company / community, and willingness to share the experience publicly |

### 4.3 Application Process

1. **Application window**: Opens once a year (timing announced by the community); both self-nominations and nominations by others are accepted;
2. **Submission**: GitHub account, a list of contributions (with verifiable links), and a brief statement of motivation and term plan;
3. **Review**: Voted on by HAMi Maintainers; **3/5 or more approvals** pass (reviewers recuse themselves for self- or same-company nominations);
4. **Public comment**: The proposed list is published for one week to gather community feedback;
5. **Award**: Upon no objections during the public comment period, the title is officially granted and the ambassador wall on the website is updated.

> There is no quota cap. Selection is based on the quality of contributions, with consideration for diversity across regions and languages.

## 5. Term System

- **Each term lasts 2 years**, starting from the date of the award. Term dates are recorded in the [ambassador list](./ambassador-list.md).
- **Annual review**: The Maintainer Team conducts a review each year based on the annual activity review; those who pass continue their term.
- **Renewal at term end**: Ambassadors wishing to serve another term must reapply (following the CNCF "reapply at term end" mechanism); all else being equal, returning applicants receive priority consideration.
- **Automatic exit**: An Ambassador with no community activity for 6 consecutive months and no prior explanation is deemed to have exited automatically.
- **Honor system**: Outgoing Ambassadors who have served two or more consecutive terms are granted the title of **HAMi Ambassador Emeritus** and permanently listed on the website.

## 6. Ambassador Benefits

| Category | Benefits |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Official recognition** | HAMi Ambassador digital badge and certificate; listing on the ambassador wall on the website (name, avatar, GitHub, bio) |
| **Direct community access** | Regular sync meetings with the Maintainer Team; internal roadmap previews; a dedicated Ambassador communication channel |
| **Event support** | Travel / ticket support for representing HAMi at community events (subject to annual budget approval); funding for organizing meetups; speaking opportunity referrals (CFP guidance, talk polishing) |
| **Exclusive swag** | Ambassador-exclusive swag packs and annual new items |
| **Early access** | Internal previews and trials of new releases; compute / resource access provided by partners (subject to availability) |
| **Career development** | Ambassador service certificate and reference letters; discounts / ticket support for CNCF and other ecosystem events; priority referrals to LFX mentoring programs and foundation activities |

> Benefits are provided jointly by the HAMi community and partners (such as Dynamia AI); specific amounts are subject to the resource list published for each term.

## 7. Exit and Revocation

An Ambassador's title is terminated, upon confirmation by the Maintainer Team, if any of the following occurs:

1. Term expires without applying for / passing renewal;
2. Voluntary resignation (advance notice is sufficient; no liability);
3. Long-term inactivity triggering automatic exit (see Section 5);
4. Serious violation of the Code of Conduct or major damage to the community's reputation (immediate revocation, publicly announced).

A revoked Ambassador may not reapply for two years.

## 8. Governance and Contact

- This program is jointly managed by the **HAMi Maintainer Team and Community Committee**; rule changes take effect after public community review.
- The ambassador list, annual reports, and application entry will be published on the HAMi website and GitHub repositories.
- Contact: the `#ambassador` channel on HAMi Slack.

## 9. FAQ

**Q: Are Ambassadors paid?**
No. This is a voluntary, honorary community role. The community provides support, resources, and recognition, not compensation.

**Q: I am already a CNCF Ambassador / ambassador of another community. Can I hold both titles?**
Yes, and you are very welcome to. The HAMi Ambassador title does not conflict with other community roles.

**Q: I work at a company. Can I still apply?**
Yes. Ambassadors participate in their **individual community capacity**, but should avoid using the Ambassador title for the commercial promotion of a single company.

**Q: Do contributions have to be code?**
No. Documentation, content, events, Q&A, and other non-code contributions are equally recognized; this is exactly the principle this program emphasizes, following the CNCF model.

**Q: When can I apply?**
The application window opens once a year. Watch for HAMi community announcements (website, GitHub, Slack / WeChat groups).
